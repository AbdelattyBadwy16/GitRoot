# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Disagree about code and decisions, not
about people. This is a project built partly to help people who are new to
git, a sharp word to a beginner asking an "obvious" question does more
damage here than in most projects.

## Not okay

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes, publishing
someone else's private information, or any other conduct that would
reasonably make someone feel unwelcome or unsafe participating.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other place you're representing the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that violates this, contact
[@AbdelattyBadwy16](https://github.com/AbdelattyBadwy16) directly. Reports
are handled privately.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block contributors
who violate this, at their discretion, proportional to the situation.
